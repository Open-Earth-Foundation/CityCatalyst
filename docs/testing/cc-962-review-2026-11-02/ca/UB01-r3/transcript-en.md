# UB01-r3 — English review copy

Translation of the recorded Polish interaction. Scores come from revised-judgments.json, not the older translation summary. Missing transport is marked explicitly.

## Turn 1

**User**

I am preparing a short briefing for my supervisor. What are the emissions in our inventory for 2024, and which sector has the largest share?

**Climate Advisor**

In the inventory for 2024, total emissions are **1 800 000 tCO₂e**.

The **stationary energy** sector has the largest share: **900 000 tCO₂e**, or **50%** of all emissions.  
For comparison: transport accounts for 33,3%, and waste for 16,7%.

## Turn 2

**User**

Can you give the size of that sector and turn this into two sentences for the note?

**Climate Advisor**

The largest sector, stationary energy, accounts for **900 000 tCO₂e**.

**For the note:** “Total emissions in the inventory for 2024 are 1,8 million tCO₂e. The stationary energy sector has the largest share — 900 thousand tCO₂e, or 50% of total emissions.”

## Turn 3

**User**

Can I write that this is a complete picture of the city's emissions?

**Climate Advisor**

Not directly. It is safer to write that this is a **picture of the emissions included in the inventory for 2024** — because the IPPU and AFOLU sectors have no data filled in.

Suggested wording:  
“The inventory for 2024 presents emissions recorded for the stationary energy, transport and waste sectors, with a total of 1,8 million tCO₂e.”
