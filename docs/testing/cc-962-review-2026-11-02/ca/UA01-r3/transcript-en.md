## Turn 1

**User**

Good morning, I am starting to work with CityCatalyst. I would like to prepare our city's emissions inventory for 2025. Where should I start, and can you create it for me?

**Climate Advisor**

Good morning! To prepare the city's emissions inventory for **2025**, start with:

1. **Creating a new inventory** in CityCatalyst  
   Select the city, reporting year **2025**, inventory type, and the GWP factor used.

2. **Establishing boundaries and methodology**  
   Define the city's administrative boundaries, the sectors to include, and a method consistent with GPC, if required.

3. **Collecting activity data**  
   These usually include, among other things:
   - electricity and fuel consumption in buildings,
   - road and public transport,
   - waste and wastewater,
   - industry, if it falls within the inventory scope,
   - local renewable energy sources.

4. **Adding data sources and emission factors**  
   For each data item, it is worth recording the source, data year, units, coverage, and the emission factor applied.

5. **Checking completeness and results**  
   After entering the data, verify missing sectors, total emissions, and the largest emission sources.

I cannot directly create the inventory on your behalf from within this conversation, but I can guide you step by step through configuration and help prepare a list of the data needed.  

To start: have you already selected a city in CityCatalyst, and do you have access to data for 2025, e.g. from energy, transport, and waste management operators?

## Turn 2

**User**

What should I select in the settings, and where can I then check whether 2025 is actually already available?

**Climate Advisor**

In the new inventory settings, select primarily:

- **City** – the correct local government unit and its administrative boundaries.
- **Inventory year:** **2025**.
- **Inventory type / reporting scope** – consistent with the city's reporting purpose; if you are preparing a standard city inventory, use an approach consistent with **GPC**.
- **GWP (global warming potential)** – select one set of factors and apply it consistently throughout the inventory. If you have program or funder requirements, follow them.
- **Boundaries** – the city's administrative boundaries and clearly defined sources and sectors covered by the calculations.
- **Currency and units** – preferably consistent with local source data; the system will convert them where configured.

To later confirm that the inventory for 2025 is available:

1. Open the inventory list for the city.
2. Search or filter by the year **2025**.
3. Check whether an entry “[city] – 2025” appears in the list.
4. Open it and check sector completeness status and inventory metadata.

If 2025 is not listed, this usually means the inventory has not yet been created or you do not have permission to access it. After creating it, you can gradually fill in sector data and check progress in the status view.
