## Turn 1

**User**

Good morning, I am starting to work with CityCatalyst. I would like to prepare our city's emissions inventory for 2025. Where should I start, and can you create it for me?

**Climate Advisor**

Good morning! I can guide you through preparing the inventory step by step, but I cannot create a new inventory in the system myself.

To start in CityCatalyst:

1. **Create an inventory for 2025** and select the city.
2. **Define the inventory boundaries** — usually the city's administrative boundaries and an approach consistent with GPC, if you use that standard.
3. **Select the GWP factor and methodology**, in accordance with your reporting requirements.
4. **Collect activity data for 2025** by sector, primarily:
   - stationary energy: electricity, gas, heat, fuels;
   - transport: fuels, road traffic, public transport;
   - waste: solid waste and wastewater;
   - industrial processes, agriculture, and land use — if relevant and within scope.
5. **Add data sources and documentation**: data owner, period, units, collection method, and assumptions.
6. **Check completeness and results** — after entering the data, we can review gaps, dominant sectors, and emission sources together.

It is also worth establishing right away whether data for the whole of 2025 are already available. If not, you can start the inventory structure and fill in sectors as data arrive.

If you create an inventory for the city and 2025, tell me the city's name — I will help you plan the data sector by sector and check progress and results.

## Turn 2

**User**

What should I select in the settings, and where can I then check whether 2025 is actually already available?

**Climate Advisor**

In the new inventory settings, select primarily:

- **Inventory year:** 2025  
- **City and boundaries:** the city's administrative boundaries, unless your reporting requires a different scope.  
- **Methodology / standard:** most often an approach consistent with **GPC** for city inventories, unless you are subject to a different standard.  
- **GWP:** select the set of factors required by your reporting program or used in previous inventories to maintain comparability between years.  
- **Gases and sectors:** retain full coverage unless you have an agreed limited reporting scope.

It is worth checking whether 2025 is “available” at two levels:

1. **In the inventory creation form**  
   If 2025 can be selected from the list, you can create an inventory for it in CityCatalyst.

2. **In the source data after creating the inventory**  
   Go to the source data section and check for each sector:
   - **the year or period covered by the data**,
   - whether the data cover the full period from January to December 2025,
   - sector completeness status and missing data.

The ability to select 2025 alone does not yet mean that all data are ready. Most often, energy data and some transport data are available first, while waste, wastewater, or some fuel data appear later.

If you tell me what reporting you are preparing the inventory for (e.g. a local climate plan, CDP, GCoM), I will help select the methodology and GWP.
