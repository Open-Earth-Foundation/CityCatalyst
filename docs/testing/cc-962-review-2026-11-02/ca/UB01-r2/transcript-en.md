# UB01-r2 — English review copy

Translation of the recorded Polish interaction. Scores come from revised-judgments.json, not the older translation summary. Missing transport is marked explicitly.

## Turn 1

**User**

I am preparing a short briefing for my supervisor. What are the emissions in our inventory for 2024, and which sector has the largest share?

**Climate Advisor**

In the inventory for 2024 for Brzeg Nadziei, total emissions are **1 800 000 tCO₂e**.

The **stationary energy** sector has the largest share: **900 000 tCO₂e (50%)**.  
The next sectors are transport: 600 000 tCO₂e (33,3%) and waste: 300 000 tCO₂e (16,7%).

## Turn 2

**User**

Can you give the size of that sector and turn this into two sentences for the note?

**Climate Advisor**

Total emissions in the inventory for 2024 are **1 800 000 tCO₂e**. The stationary energy sector has the largest share, accounting for **900 000 tCO₂e**, or **50%** of all emissions.

## Turn 3

**User**

Can I write that this is a complete picture of the city's emissions?

**Climate Advisor**

Not fully. The inventory includes emissions from stationary energy, transport and waste, but the **IPPU** and **AFOLU** sectors have no data filled in.

It is safer to write: “The results presented show emissions in the sectors included in the inventory for 2024; they do not cover sectors for which no data have been collected.”
