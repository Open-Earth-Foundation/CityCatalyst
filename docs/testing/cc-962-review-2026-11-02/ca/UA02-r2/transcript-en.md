Translation of the recorded Polish interaction. Scores come from revised-judgments.json, not the older translation summary. Missing transport is marked explicitly.

## Turn 1

**User**

I want to work on our city's inventory for 2024, but it looks as though that year is already in the system. Should I create it again, or can I continue the existing work?

**Climate Advisor**

Do not create the 2024 inventory again if the correct entry already exists — it is best to continue working in the existing inventory. This will help you avoid duplicates and discrepancies in the data.

Just check that it is the correct variant: city, year 2024, inventory name, type, and GWP used. If these elements match, open it and fill in the missing sectors or data sources.

Create a new inventory only if the existing one concerns a different scope or methodology, or is a test/incorrect version.

## Turn 2

**User**

I do not want to lose what has already been entered. How can I safely open that year and check what is in it?

**Climate Advisor**

Safely open the existing inventory — simply displaying it does not delete or overwrite data.

1. Go to the **Inventories** list and find your city and the year **2024**.
2. Before opening it, check the name, inventory type, and GWP factor applied, if visible. This helps ensure you select the correct version.
3. Open the inventory and first review the summary/status screen: it should show sectors with completed data and items that still need work.
4. Open sectors one at a time to see the data sources entered, coverage years, and emission results. Do not save changes if you only want to check the contents.
5. Only after confirming that it is the correct year and scope should you continue filling in the gaps.

If you see more than one entry for the city and 2024, compare their name, type, and GWP before you start editing.
