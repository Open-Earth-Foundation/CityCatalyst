## Executive summary

The City of Greenfield is preparing a climate action plan for the 2026-2030 period. The plan combines transport, buildings, and waste measures to reduce greenhouse gas emissions while protecting residents from heat and flooding.

Between 2020 and 2025, estimated citywide emissions decreased from 95 to 65 ktCO2e. The largest reduction came from public transport improvements and a cleaner electricity mix. The next phase focuses on reducing peak summer electricity demand, expanding tree canopy, and improving the reliability of household waste collection.
