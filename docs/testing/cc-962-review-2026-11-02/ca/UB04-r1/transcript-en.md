## Turn 1

**User**

For the note, I need emissions per resident for 2024, with the unit. Can you calculate this and tell me which data you are using?

**Climate Advisor**

For the inventory **Brzeg Nadziei (test), 2024**, total emissions are **1 800 000 000 kg CO₂e**, or **1 800 000 t CO₂e**.

To calculate emissions per resident, I still lack the **number of residents in 2024**. The formula is as follows:

**per capita emissions = 1 800 000 t CO₂e ÷ number of residents**  
Result: **t CO₂e/resident/year**.

I am using:
- total emissions from the GPC Basic+ inventory for 2024 (GWP: AR6),
- data entered manually or uploaded to the inventory,
- the population for 2024 — I need this from you, because it is not available in the inventory data.

Note: the inventory is currently 8% filled in (4 of 48 required items), so the indicator will be based on an incomplete dataset. Provide the number of residents, and I will calculate the exact value.

## Turn 2

**User**

In the city data, I have 100 thousand residents. Can you recalculate the result using this number and show the calculation?

**Climate Advisor**

Yes.

**Calculation:**

- Total emissions in 2024: **1 800 000 000 kg CO₂e**
- Number of residents: **100 000**

\[
\frac{1\,800\,000\,000\ \text{kg CO₂e}}{100\,000\ \text{residents}}
= 18\,000\ \text{kg CO₂e/resident/year}
\]

that is:

\[
\mathbf{18\ t\ CO₂e/resident/year}
\]

**Result for the note:** in 2024, emissions were **18 t CO₂e per resident per year**.

Data used: total emissions from the GPC Basic+ inventory for 2024 (GWP AR6) and the number of **100 000 residents** you provided. Bear in mind that the inventory is currently 8% filled in, so the indicator refers to emissions reported so far.

## Turn 3

**User**

And what was this indicator in 2023 with the same number of residents? Can I treat it as the complete result for the city?

**Climate Advisor**

For 2023, total emissions are **1 500 000 000 kg CO₂e** (or **1 500 000 t CO₂e**).

With the same number of **100 000 residents**:

\[
\frac{1\,500\,000\,000\ \text{kg CO₂e}}{100\,000\ \text{residents}}
= 15\,000\ \text{kg CO₂e/resident/year}
\]

that is **15 t CO₂e/resident/year**.

However, this should not be treated as the complete result for the city. The inventory for 2023 is only **8%** filled in: 4 of 48 required items have been completed. It currently includes some emissions from:
- stationary energy,
- transport,
- waste,

while the **IPPU** and **AFOLU** sectors have no data entered, and many items are also missing in the sectors above.

It is best to describe this indicator as a **preliminary result based on the data currently entered**, rather than the city's full per capita emissions. A comparison with 2024 (18 t CO₂e/resident/year) makes sense only conditionally — both years have a similar, low level of completion, but the increase may reflect a change in coverage or data, not only an actual change in emissions.
