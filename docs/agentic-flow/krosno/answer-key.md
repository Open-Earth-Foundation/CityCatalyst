The chart values in the 2023 report are read from extracted chart labels, so the year-to-value pairing should be eyeballed in the PDF.

- **Main sector** (`sectors.main`, confidence medium): By money the largest sector in Annex E is Innovative energy infrastructure (EUR 86,000,000, 53.5%), not Residential buildings (EUR 27,183,000). Which one was ticked as 'main' in the online form is not visible in the files.

- **Expected renewable energy production** (`impacts.renewable_production_gwh`, confidence medium): Possible double counting: 7 GWh of the PV electricity feeds the electrolyser and the hydrogen CHP output (3.2 GWh) is counted again; heat storage (4.5 GWh) is counted as generation. 21 MWp giving 21 GWh assumes 1,000 kWh/kWp.

- **Emission reduction target** (`plan.target`, confidence medium): A 2019 update still giving 2020 as target year looks like text left from the 2015 plan; the chapters for other municipalities in the same document say 'by 2025' (pp. 280, 343). A native reader should confirm which year applies to Krosno.
- **Baseline emissions** (`plan.baseline_emissions`, confidence medium): Year (2013 header vs 2018 data), total (223,990.33 vs 445,076.32) and the wood emission factor all need a human decision. The 223,990.33 figure is reconstructed from cells split across text lines on pp. 129-130. On p. 93 the same plan says biomass has a zero CO2 balance.

## Known problems in the sources

- **Two codes are not from any file.** The NUTS 3 code (PL821) and the LAU code (1861011) in the key come from memory and must be checked against the Eurostat table.
- **Population differs between documents** for the same year: 46,291 (local development plan, p.7) and 45,147 (low-emission plan, p.61).
- **The application contradicts itself on the goal:** climate neutrality by 2040 in item 2.3, by 2050 in items 2.5 and 3.2.
- **The emissions inventory** uses 2018 data under a table headed 2013, counts wood as emitting CO2 (36.5% of the total) although the same plan calls biomass CO2-neutral, and the plan's own target is a 20% cut by 2020.
- **Annex D baselines do not match the plan**, most sharply residential district heat (170 GWh in the annex, about 59 GWh of total network sales in the plan). An engineer should look at this before the energy figures are used as a reference.
- **No council resolution** adopting any of the three plans was found in the files.
- **The low-emission plan is a joint plan** for Krosno and six neighbouring municipalities. The Krosno chapter is pages 47 to 153.
