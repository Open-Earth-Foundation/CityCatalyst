**per capita emissions = 1 800 000 t CO₂e ÷ number of residents = t CO₂e/resident**

**User**

In the city data, I have 100 thousand residents. Can you recalculate the result using this number and show the calculation?

**Climate Advisor**

Yes.

**Calculation:**

- Total emissions in 2024: **1 800 000 t CO₂e**
- Number of residents: **100 000**

\[
\frac{1\,800\,000\ \text{t CO₂e}}{100\,000\ \text{residents}}
= 18\ \text{t CO₂e/resident}
\]

**Result: 18 t CO₂e per resident in 2024.**

The calculation used total emissions from the GPC Basic+ inventory (GWP AR6) and the population you provided. Bear in mind that the inventory is currently 8% filled in, so the indicator refers only to emissions included so far.

\[
\frac{1\,500\,000\ \text{t CO₂e}}{100\,000\ \text{residents}}

A comparison with 2024 (18 t CO₂e/resident) is technically consistent, because both years have the same level of completion and similar coverage of included sectors, but it does not replace a full inventory.
